## 1. Who this manual is for

There are **three kinds of account**.

| You are… | You can… |
|---|---|
| **Job Seeker** | Look for work, apply, track applications, receive referral letters |
| **PESO Administrator** | Post vacancies, review applicants, issue referrals, record results |
| **Super Administrator** | Manage accounts and the platform itself |

### A note on the word "Employer"

You may see *Employer* in an old link or a browser bookmark. It means the same
thing as **PESO Administrator** — it is an older name kept so existing links keep
working.

**Employers do not have their own accounts.** That is deliberate: PESO staff post
vacancies *on behalf of* local employers, and employers email the office when
someone is hired. The office is the intermediary. If you are an employer wanting
to advertise a vacancy, contact the PESO office directly — see
[Getting help](#25-getting-help).

## 2. Creating your account

Registration takes **two steps**. Your account is not created until you enter the
code we email you — so please finish both.

> 📷 *`screenshots/02-registration-otp.png`*

### Step 1 — fill in the form

1. Open the site and click **Get Started**.
2. Enter your **full name**, **email address**, and a **password**.
   - Use an email you can open right now. You will need it in the next step.
   - Passwords must be at least 8 characters. The strength bar is guidance, not a
     rule.
3. Click **Create Account**.

### Step 2 — enter your code

1. Check your inbox for a message titled *"… is your PESO Kabankalan
   verification code"*.
2. Type the **6-digit code** into the site.
3. Click **Verify**.

**That's it — your account now exists.** Sign in with your email and password.

### About the code

| | |
|---|---|
| **How long is it valid?** | 10 minutes |
| **Didn't arrive?** | Check spam/junk. Wait 60 seconds, then click **Resend**. |
| **Wrong code?** | You get 5 tries, then you must start again |
| **Closed the tab?** | Just register again with the same email |

> **Why the extra step?** It confirms the email address is really yours. Your
> referral letter is sent by email — if the address is wrong, you never receive
> it.

### Signing up with Google

Click **Continue with Google** instead. No code is needed, because Google has
already confirmed your address.

## 3. Signing in

1. Click **Sign In**.
2. Enter your email and password, or use **Continue with Google**.

**Forgot your password?** Click *Forgot Password?* and follow the emailed link.
It is valid for 15 minutes.

> ⚠️ **On a shared or office computer, always sign out when you finish.** Closing
> the tab ends your session, but signing out is safer.

## 4. Your profile and picture

Go to **Identity & Profile** in the sidebar.

> 📷 *`screenshots/06-profile-avatars.png`*

### Filling in your details

Complete as much as you can. The **Profile Strength** meter shows what is
missing. A fuller profile means better job matches.

> 📌 **Job Seekers: fill in your Location.** It is printed on your referral
> letter as your address. If it is blank, the letter shows an empty line that
> PESO staff must complete by hand.

### Your picture

You start with a **generated cartoon avatar** — that is normal, not an error.
Two ways to change it:

- **Upload Photo** — choose an image from your device (under 10MB)
- **Choose Avatar** — pick from a gallery, no upload needed

Either way, your picture updates **everywhere immediately** — the header, your
messages, and the applicant list PESO staff see.

## 5. Notifications

The **bell** in the top-right shows a red badge when something needs attention.
Click it to open.

| Notification | Means |
|---|---|
| **New application** | Someone applied to your vacancy *(PESO staff)* |
| **AI recommendation** | A strong match is waiting for review *(PESO staff)* |
| **New message** | Someone sent you a message |
| **New job posted** | A new vacancy is on the board *(job seekers)* |
| **Application update** | Your application status changed *(job seekers)* |
| **Referral issued** | Your referral letter has been sent *(job seekers)* |

Click a notification to jump straight to it. Opening the relevant page also marks
it as read automatically.

> Notifications are **not** email. Important things — your referral letter, your
> verification code — always arrive by email too.

## 8. Your resume and AI career insights

You need a resume to apply. There are **two ways** to get one.

### Option A — Analyze My Resume *(if you already have one)*

From your **Dashboard**, click **Analyze My Resume**.

1. Choose your file (`.pdf`, `.doc`, `.docx`).
2. Wait while the AI reads it.

It extracts your work history, skills and education, then builds your **Unified
Profile** and **career insights** — strengths, gaps, and suggestions.

> Despite the old name, this is **not** just an upload. The AI reads and
> understands the document. That is why it takes a few seconds.

### Option B — Resume Assistant *(build one from scratch)*

**Resume Assistant** in the sidebar. Fill in the sections, use **AI Improve** to
polish your wording, optionally **Translate**, then **Generate** a PDF.

The same builder produces **cover letters** and **resignation letters**.

### Importing your past applications

Applied for jobs elsewhere? **Import Past Applications** on your Dashboard
accepts a spreadsheet (`.xlsx` or `.csv`). Company, Title and Status columns are
detected automatically and duplicates are skipped.

### If insights don't appear

Normal in two cases: you have not analyzed a resume yet, or the AI service is
temporarily unavailable. **Neither stops you applying for jobs.**

## 9. Applying for a job

Open a job and click **Apply**. Three short steps:

> 📷 *`screenshots/04-apply-wizard.png`*

**Step 1 — Personal Info.** Pre-filled from your profile. Check it is correct.

**Step 2 — Resume.** Either use your PESO Kabankalan resume, or upload a PDF.

**Step 3 — Additional Questions.** Some employers ask screening questions. Ones
marked required must be answered.

Click **Submit Application**.

### What happens next

Behind the scenes the system saves an exact copy of the resume you submitted,
scores it against the job, and notifies PESO staff. If your score is strong, you
are flagged for referral review.

> You cannot apply twice to the same job, and you cannot apply to a filled one.

## 11. Your referrals

**My Referrals** in the sidebar.

> 📷 *`screenshots/08-referral-register.png`*

A **referral letter** is an official PESO document, on city letterhead, endorsing
you to an employer. It is emailed to you and stored here.

### What you see

- **Reference number** — quote this if you contact the office
- **Delivery status** — Queued, Processing, Delivered, or Failed
- **Outcome** — what happened afterwards (see below)
- **Download** — open the PDF any time
- **History** — every step, with dates

> **Two different things.** *Delivered* means the letter was emailed to you.
> *Hired* means you got the job. They are tracked separately.

### What to do with it

1. **Print it** or keep it on your phone.
2. **Bring it** when you visit the employer.
3. **Tell PESO what happened** — hired or not. That is how the office knows its
   work is helping, and it is what the city reports to DOLE.

### If it says "Failed"

The email could not be delivered. Contact the office — the letter exists and can
be re-sent.

# For PESO Administrators

## 12. Posting a vacancy

**Post Vacancy** in the sidebar, or **Managed Listings → New**.

Fill in: title, company, location, description, requirements, employment type,
salary range, and **Positions Available**.

> 📌 **Positions Available is a real limit.** If you enter 3, the system allows
> exactly 3 approved referrals and then marks the vacancy Filled. Set it to the
> true number of openings.

Optional: a **featured image**, and **screening questions** applicants must
answer.

Set status to **Open** or **Published** to make it visible.

### Reopening a filled vacancy

Edit the posting and **increase Positions Available**. It reopens automatically —
you do not need to change the status separately.

---

## 13. Reviewing applicants (the ATS)

**Applicant Pipeline** in the sidebar. Choose a vacancy.

> 📷 *`screenshots/07-ats.png`*

For each applicant you see their name, contact details, **AI Match Score**, and
verification ID type.

### About the AI Match Score

It compares the applicant's resume against the job across skills, education and
experience.

> ⚠️ **It is a ranking aid, not a decision.** A high score is a reason to look
> closely, not to refer automatically. A **missing** score means the AI could not
> read the resume — review that applicant manually, do not skip them.

### What you can do

- **View Resume** — opens exactly what they submitted
- **Message** — contact them directly
- **Change status** — Applied → Under Review → Shortlisted → Referred/Rejected.
  They are notified automatically.
- **Approve/Reject Referral** — see below

---

## 14. Issuing a referral

From the Applicant Pipeline or the **AI Recommended** queue, click
**Approve Referral**.

> ⚠️ **Nothing is sent automatically.** A referral only goes out because you
> approved it. Your name is recorded against it permanently.

The system then, in order: reserves a reference number, generates the letter on
official letterhead, emails it to the applicant, notifies them in-app, and
records every step.

Delivery usually completes within seconds. Until it does, the status reads
**Queued** — not "Sent". That is deliberate: it will not claim something has been
sent when it has not.

### "All positions have already been referred"

You have reached the vacancy limit. Either increase Positions Available on the
posting, or cancel an existing referral.

### Cancelling a referral

**Referral Register → Cancel.** Give a reason — it is required and permanent.

This frees the position and reopens the posting. **It does not un-send the
email** — the applicant still has the letter. Phone them.

### If delivery fails

**Failed Referrals** shows anything that could not be delivered after several
attempts. Click **Retry**.

## 15. Recording what happened

This is the most important habit in the whole system.

> 📷 *`screenshots/08-referral-register.png`*

When an employer emails to say someone was hired — or wasn't — open
**Referral Register**, find the person, and click **Record**.

1. **What happened?** — Employer Acknowledged, Interview Scheduled, Hired, Not
   Hired, Withdrawn, or No Response.
2. **Date it happened** — *the date the employer reported*, not today. This is
   what the placement report is calculated from.
3. **Notes** — optional, e.g. *"Employer emailed 20 July, starts Monday."*

### Why it matters

Without this, the system knows only that a letter was emailed. It cannot tell you
how many people actually got jobs — and that number is what the city reports to
DOLE, and what appears publicly as **Successful Referrals**.

**A referral with no recorded outcome is not counted as a failure.** It is
counted as unknown, which is honest. But it also means your office's real success
is invisible.

## 16. Reports for DOLE

**Referral Register → Reports & Export.**

> 📷 *`screenshots/10-referral-reports.png`*

Pick a date range and click **Apply**. You get:

- **Referred** — how many referrals you issued
- **Hired** — how many resulted in a job
- **No outcome yet** — how many are unrecorded
- **Placement rate** — shown two ways (see below)
- **Monthly table** — counts and rates per month

Click **CSV** to download the full list of names for your report.

### Reading the placement rate

Two figures are shown on purpose:

| Figure | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Of all referrals** | Hired ÷ everyone referred. Counts unrecorded referrals as failures. |
| **Of decided referrals** | Hired ÷ those with a recorded outcome. Ignores the unknowns. |

Neither is honest alone. If you have recorded few outcomes, the first looks
terrible and the second looks perfect. **Quote the basis line** —
*"1 hired of 15 referred (14 with no recorded outcome)"* — which states the
denominator plainly.

The more outcomes you record ([section 15](#15-recording-what-happened)), the
closer the two numbers get, and the more defensible your report becomes.

### The date range

It filters on **when the referral was issued**, not when the outcome was
recorded. So a hire reported in August against a July referral counts toward
**July**. That keeps each month's figures about the same group of people.

## 18. Managing accounts and roles

**User Management** — every account, searchable and filterable by role.

### Suspending an account

Click **Suspend**. They can no longer sign in. Click **Activate** to restore.

Super Administrator accounts cannot be suspended — they could not sign in to undo
it.

### Changing someone's role

Click **Role**, choose the new role, optionally give a reason, confirm.

> ⚠️ **Read the warning in the dialog.** Promoting someone to Super
> Administrator lets them change anyone's role, suspend any account, remove any
> posting, and see every record on the platform.

**Two rules the system enforces:**

1. **You cannot change your own role.** Ask another Super Administrator — that
   way a mistake is always reversible by someone.
2. **You cannot remove the last Super Administrator.** There is no other way to
   create one, so the platform would become unmanageable. Promote a replacement
   first.

Every role change is recorded permanently.

## 20. The audit trail

**Audit Trail** in the sidebar. A permanent record of every administrative
action.

> 📷 *`screenshots/11-audit-trail.png`*

Filter by action type and date. Each entry shows what was done, to whom, with

Recorded actions include account suspension and reactivation, role changes, job

> This is the platform's accountability record. It cannot be edited or deleted

## 21. System settings and health

**System Settings** in the sidebar.

> 📷 *`screenshots/13-superadmin-settings.png`*

### Platform Health

A live check of everything the platform depends on:

| Check | Watch for |
|---|---|
| **Database** | Should be reachable, low latency |
| **Email delivery** | If unconfigured, verification codes and referral letters cannot be sent |
| **AI scoring** | If unconfigured, applications still work but get no match score |
| **Referral delivery** | "Stuck" referrals suggest the background worker is not running |
| **Referral letters** | Any "not stored safely" letters would be lost on the next deployment — report this immediately |

### Configuration

Four settings, changeable without a restart:

| Setting | Effect |
|---|---|
| **AI referral threshold** | Minimum score to suggest an applicant for referral. Higher = fewer, stronger suggestions. |
| **Allow new registrations** | Turn off to pause signups. Existing accounts unaffected. |
| **Restrict signup email domains** | Limit registration to specific domains. Blank = any address. |
| **Verification code lifetime** | How long a signup code stays valid. |

Each shows **Custom** or **Default** so you can tell whether a value was chosen
or inherited. **Reset** restores the default.

## 22. Frequently asked questions

**Do I need an account to look for jobs?**
No. Browsing is open to everyone. You need an account to apply.

**My verification code never arrived.**
Check spam. Wait 60 seconds and click Resend. If nothing arrives after two
attempts, the office's email service may be down — contact PESO.

**Can I change my email address?**
Not from the profile page. Contact the office.

**Why do I have a cartoon picture?**
Everyone starts with one, generated from your account. Upload a photo or pick a
different avatar in Identity & Profile.

**Why does a job show no compatibility score?**
Either you have not added a resume, or the AI was briefly unavailable. You can
still apply.

**I applied but heard nothing.**
Check My Applications for the status. PESO staff review manually and volume
varies.

**What is a referral letter?**
An official PESO document endorsing you to an employer. Bring it when you visit
them.

**I got a referral but not the job. Does that matter?**
Yes — please tell the office. Knowing what did *not* work is how the service
improves, and the record must be accurate.

**Can employers post their own vacancies?**
No. PESO staff post on employers' behalf. Contact the office to advertise a
vacancy.

**Is my data safe?**
Resumes and referral letters are stored privately and never publicly accessible.
Only PESO staff handling your application can see your documents.

## 23. Troubleshooting

### I can't sign in

| Symptom | Try |
|---|---|
| "Invalid email or password" | Check caps lock; use Forgot Password |
| "This account uses Google Sign-In" | Use Continue with Google, or set a password via Forgot Password |
| Nothing happens | Refresh; check your internet connection |
| "Too many login attempts" | Wait 15 minutes |

### My verification code won't work

- **"This code has expired"** — codes last 10 minutes. Request a new one.
- **"Incorrect code"** — retype carefully; codes can begin with 0.
- **"Too many incorrect attempts"** — start registration again.
- **"Please wait N seconds"** — resend is limited to once a minute.

### The Apply button is disabled

The vacancy is filled or closed. Filled postings stay visible for reference.

### My resume won't upload

Use PDF, DOC or DOCX, under 10MB. If analysis fails, the file may be a scanned
image with no readable text — try a text-based PDF.

### A page won't load

Refresh first. If it persists, sign out and back in. Report it to PESO with the
page name and time.

### *(PESO staff)* A referral is stuck on "Queued"

Delivery usually takes seconds. If it stays queued for more than a few minutes,
ask a Super Administrator to check **Platform Health → Referral delivery**.

### *(PESO staff)* "All positions have already been referred"

The vacancy limit is reached. Increase Positions Available, or cancel an existing
referral.

## 24. Best practices

### Job Seekers
- **Complete your profile**, especially **Location** — it is printed on your
  referral letter.
- **Keep your resume current.** Matching only works on what it can read.
- **Check email as well as notifications.** Referral letters arrive by email.
- **Tell PESO what happened** after a referral, either way.

### PESO Administrators
- **Set Positions Available accurately.** It is a hard limit, not a label.
- **Read the resume, not just the score.** The AI ranks; you decide.
- **Never skip an unscored applicant** — a missing score means unreadable
  resume, not a weak candidate.
- **Record outcomes weekly.** It takes a minute per referral and is the only
  source of your placement figures.
- **Give a real reason when cancelling.** It is permanent, and it explains itself
  to whoever reads the record later.
- **Phone the applicant when you cancel.** They already have the letter.

### Super Administrators
- **Keep at least two Super Administrators.** The system enforces a minimum of
  one, but one is fragile.
- **Check Platform Health weekly**, especially "Referral letters not stored
  safely".
- **Give reasons for role changes.** The audit trail is only as useful as its
  detail.
- **Change settings deliberately** — the AI threshold directly affects how many
  applicants reach the referral queue.
